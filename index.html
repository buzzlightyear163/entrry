07 · faq

%man entry

is this financial advice?

No. ENTRY shows what public wallets did on-chain. It never tells you to buy, and a KOL buying is not a reason to.

whose wallets are these?

Only wallets a KOL has shown publicly, or that are publicly tied to them. Every row links to the transaction so anyone can check it. If a wallet is wrong, it comes off the list.

does the desk trade for me today?

No. The feed and the gap check are live. The desk is in beta: you can arm it, but nothing executes and no funds move until beta opens.

why holders only?

The feed is the product and $ENTRY is the key. Everyone can see that a KOL moved. Holders see what they moved into.

what can't it see?

It reads trades on the pump.fun curve from wallets on the list. A KOL buying from a fresh wallet, on another venue or through a bundle won't show. And getting in at their price doesn't stop them from selling first.

will entry DM me?

Never. Anyone who does is not us.
